OVERVIEW

About the App

Hardware Marketplace is a mobile application designed to make browsing and listing hardware products easier and more organized.

Users can post hardware items, explore available products, search by name or category, view product information, and manage their own listings in one convenient application.

02
WHAT YOU CAN DO

Key Features

01

Post Hardware Products

Add product names, categories, brands, prices, stock, conditions, descriptions, and contact information.

02

Browse and Search

Find hardware products quickly using search and category filters.

03

Edit Product Listings

Update product details, prices, descriptions, and available stock.

04

Delete Products

Remove listings that are no longer needed.

05

Track Product Stock

Display stock quantities and identify products that are out of stock.

06

Cloud Database

Store and retrieve hardware product listings using Firebase Firestore.
